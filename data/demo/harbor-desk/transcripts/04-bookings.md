# Harbor Desk discovery 04 — Reservation window

Mira (analyst): Shall we use the camera example? We can skip the weather this time, although I see another umbrella by your desk.
Jun (member representative): It is drying. Yes, the camera booking is why I am here. Members plan workshops earlier than the portal lets them reserve.
Mira (analyst): The baseline advance window is fourteen calendar days for every role, and the maximum duration is three days.
Jun (member representative): Allow reservations to start up to thirty calendar days after submission.
Mira (analyst): That conflicts with the fourteen-day limit. Are you proposing a new duration too?
Jun (member representative): No. Keep the maximum duration at three consecutive calendar days.
Nora (cooperative lead): The thirty-day idea needs approval because it changes how early people can claim equipment. Do not hide it as a configuration tweak.
Mira (analyst): What happens if the requested slot is already taken?
Jun (member representative): Let members subscribe to an email when a selected item and date interval become available.
Mira (analyst): A waiting-list style notification is new. Is this automatic reservation when the slot opens?
Jun (member representative): Definitely not. Send an email with a portal link. The member must still submit a reservation, and another person may get there first.
Nora (cooperative lead): I first said book every Thursday, but recurring reservations can wait. Do not create a recurring-booking story from that aside.
Mira (analyst): Should subscriptions have an expiry or an unsubscribe action?
Jun (member representative): Yes, members must be able to unsubscribe. I do not know the expiry rule; please ask us before settling it.
Mira (analyst): We can capture the feature and leave the expiry decision open. Any need for SMS?
Jun (member representative): No, email only. I want the same familiar channel, not another notification system.
Nora (cooperative lead): Good. The practical test for me is cancelling a reservation, getting the email as a subscriber and still having to book manually. We will review the thirty-day rule as a separate policy change.
