Mira (analyst): Did you find the spare keyboard? Last time we were discussing navigation without a mouse.

Mira (analyst): That repeats the accessibility baseline, which is useful to confirm. We should test it rather than treat it as extra scope.

Mira (analyst): Does it replace the default alphabetical ordering?
Owen (coordinator): No. Load the saved category when I return, keep alphabetical ordering, and let me clear the preset. One preset is plenty.
Jun (member representative): The reservation form should be faster.
Mira (analyst): Faster to load, fewer actions to submit, or a shorter server response? How would we measure success?
Jun (member representative): I cannot say yet. We should watch a member do it. Do not invent a two-second target just because I used the word faster.
Mira (analyst): Agreed. Any visual changes needed for status?
Jun (member representative): Keep a text label next to any status color. I know we agreed that before; I am repeating it because the printout lost the colors.
Owen (coordinator): And do not build a whole design system for the saved preset. A clear save and clear control is enough.
Mira (analyst): We will keep the new persistent category choice separate from baseline accessibility and the unresolved usability target. Next time we can look at one observed workflow instead of trying to fix every annoyance at once.
