# Harbor Desk discovery 01 — Reporting handoff

Mira (analyst): Before we start, did the projector arrive back from the library? The rain was impressive this morning.
Nora (cooperative lead): It did, and the case was dry. Let us start with reports before we forget why we booked this call.
Mira (analyst): The approved document lets coordinators view reports and only administrators export them. Is the complaint about the report itself or the access rule?
Nora (cooperative lead): Both, but they are separate. Coordinators must be allowed to export reservation reports too.
Mira (analyst): That changes the administrator-only export policy. I will mark it as a conflict, not assume the change is approved. Do you still need CSV?
Nora (cooperative lead): Keep the export as UTF-8 CSV using the active filters.
Owen (coordinator): I first thought PDF because people print things, but no, forget PDF for this release. CSV is what we actually send to our internal spreadsheet.
Mira (analyst): Good, I will not turn the withdrawn PDF idea into a requirement. What is missing from the current manual process?
Owen (coordinator): At month end I keep adding up reservations by hand. Add a monthly reservation count to the report.
Mira (analyst): Do you mean one total for the selected interval, or a month-by-month breakdown? The report already has filter-matched counts above the rows.
Owen (coordinator): A month-by-month breakdown, even if the date filter spans three months. The ordinary total can stay where it is.
Nora (cooperative lead): Yes. Add a month-by-month reservation count to the report, grouped by reservation start month.
Mira (analyst): Is scheduled email needed now?
Nora (cooperative lead): Not now. We can download manually; please do not invent a scheduler because I said month end.
Owen (coordinator): One other thing: make exporting feel instant.
Mira (analyst): That needs a report size and a time target. We have not agreed either. I will leave a question rather than a performance acceptance criterion.
Nora (cooperative lead): Fine. I need to take the next call, so send the access-policy question separately. The board has not approved broader exports yet.
