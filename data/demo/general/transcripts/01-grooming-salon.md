# Fictional call — grooming salon booking app

Lena (salon owner): Sorry I'm late, the parking here is a nightmare. Did you get coffee?
Tom (product manager): All good. Let's talk about the booking app for the grooming salon.
Lena (salon owner): Customers should book a grooming slot online and pick the groomer they want.
Tom (product manager): Any limits on how far ahead?
Lena (salon owner): Up to six weeks ahead. Oh, and send an SMS reminder the day before the appointment.
Tom (product manager): SMS or email?
Lena (salon owner): Actually, make it email only, SMS costs too much for us right now.
Lena (salon owner): Customers must be able to cancel for free until 24 hours before the slot. After that they pay half the price.
Tom (product manager): Should we support dog breeds and sizes?
Lena (salon owner): Yes, the customer enters breed and size, because a large dog needs a 90 minute slot instead of 60.
Lena (salon owner): And the app should look nice, modern, you know.
Tom (product manager): We also talked about a loyalty program last time.
Lena (salon owner): Let's skip loyalty for now, maybe next year.
